Who We Are

We don't rebuild what works.
We make it work smarter.

Acadeno Technologies Private Limited is an enterprise software development and AI engineering company headquartered in Calicut, Kerala — operating from Govt Cyberpark, one of India's most advanced SEZ technology hubs.

Founded in 2025, we were built around a single, focused belief: that artificial intelligence should integrate into the tools your business already depends on — not replace them. Our team of AI specialists, full-stack engineers, and systems architects works across industries to solve specific enterprise challenges.
